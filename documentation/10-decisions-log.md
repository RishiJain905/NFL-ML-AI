| D48 | 2026-10-03 | **Game model v0 configuration** (P03 ✋; Rishi waived P03's 🧑/✋ steps for this phase). The schedule's projected QBs exist only live, never historically | The Saturday injury update (P07); a better week-1 rule from weekly rosters |
| D50 | 2026-10-03 | **Game-model baselines and metrics:**<br>• Home team always: the walk-forward home win rate, 0.5 at neutral sites.<br>• Elo: as of the game's week (Tuesday view), home field 0 at neutral sites.<br>• Market: `Φ(spread / σ_m)`, σ_m = RMSE of the margin around the spread in the training rows; the vig-free moneyline is logged as a reference.<br>• ESPN FPI is **not** a backtest baseline: only the current snapshot is collected.<br>• Ties count 0.5 in Brier, log loss and ECE, and are left out of accuracy (as in P02).
